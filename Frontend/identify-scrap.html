Tips for a clear photo

  • Place the item on a plain surface.
  • Use good light and avoid shadows.
  • Show the whole item in the photo.
  • Never handle a damaged battery for a photo.
A photo can suggest a category. A recycler checks material, condition, and weight before confirming any price.
